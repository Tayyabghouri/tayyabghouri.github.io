About

Payment systems, built and maintained in production

I'm a Full Stack .NET Developer based in Islamabad with 5 years of experience across fintech, healthcare and government systems. I currently work at Croem Pakistan on NeoGateway, a multi-tenant payment gateway, and the services around it: transaction processing with acquirers and card networks, recurring billing, payment links and merchant statements.

A lot of my work sits in the data layer: optimizing SQL queries, stored procedures and reporting modules to reduce response times, and integrating card processing APIs to improve transaction success rates.

Before Croem, I worked on the eFBR and IRIS tax automation systems at PRAL, adding real-time functionality with SignalR, and on EHR software at MTBC using React, Web API, C# and SQL.
